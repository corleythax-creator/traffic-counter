Average vehicles per frame for each hour, across everything recorded so far. Touch and slide to read each hour. Hours with no data are left blank; this fills in as capture runs through a full day.

Counts are YOLO (You Only Look Once) detections of vehicles visible in each camera snapshot, so they measure how busy the intersection looks at a moment, not how many vehicles passed. Counts include only vehicles in each camera's counting zone (the near and middle stretch of road); very distant traffic is too small to count reliably. Night frames tend to undercount. Refreshes every 2 minutes while this page is open. Source: MDOT (Mississippi Department of Transportation) traffic cameras.
