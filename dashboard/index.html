Counts are YOLO (You Only Look Once) detections of vehicles visible in each snapshot, a measure of how busy each spot looks, not how many vehicles passed. Each camera is sampled for about 4 minutes every 15 minutes (Lakeland Dr S at Treetops was recorded continuously until Sept. 18). People are counted anywhere in each frame, separately from vehicles. After dark the Jackson cameras switch to infrared and the model detects far less than is really on the road - those stretches are faded on the charts and marked on the card. The sparkline on each row is the last 15 minutes a minute at a time, with the past hour's average drawn through it as a dashed line. It deepens toward red the further a minute runs above that camera's own past hour - neutral at or below the hour, red from about half again above it - so the colour means the same thing on a busy junction and a quiet one. The line breaks at minutes with no frames rather than drawing through them, because an unsampled minute is not a quiet one. Open a row for its live video.
