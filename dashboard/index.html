Typical hour of the day

Average vehicles per frame for each hour, across everything recorded so far. Fills in as the cameras run through full days.

Counts are YOLO (You Only Look Once) detections of vehicles visible in each snapshot, a measure of how busy each spot looks, not how many vehicles passed. Each camera is sampled for about 4 minutes every 15 minutes (Lakeland Dr S at Treetops was recorded continuously until Sept. 18). People are counted anywhere in each frame, separately from vehicles. The bars on each camera show the last 15 minutes one minute at a time, with the past hour's average drawn through them as a dashed line; a blank slot is a minute with no frames, not a minute with no traffic.

Source: MDOT (Mississippi Department of Transportation) traffic cameras at mdottraffic.com.
